Privacy policy for Colour by Sums: Kids Maths

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Colour by Sums: Kids Maths is a colouring and maths game for children. It is made by Rahima Chowdhury.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What is kept on the device

The only thing the app saves is the number of stars your child has earned. This is kept on your own device so the stars are still there next time. It never leaves the device, and clearing the app's data removes it.

Read aloud

The read-aloud feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. The app does not use the microphone or record anything.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 5 to 8. Because it collects no information at all, no personal information is ever collected from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact the developer using the email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
